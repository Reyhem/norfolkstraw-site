About Norfolk Straw

We're a working farm near Attleborough, growing wheat, barley and oilseed across roughly 800 acres. The straw is a by-product of the harvest — and after nearly three decades of baling, we know how to make a bale that travels well and stores clean.

🌾

Farm-fresh bales

Baled within 48 hours of combining. Dry, golden, and properly compressed for storage and transport.

🚚

Our own lorries

Two flatbed lorries and a tipping trailer. We deliver on the day we agree — not "sometime that week".

💷

Fair, fixed pricing

Per-bale quotes that include delivery in our zone. No fuel surcharges added on the day.

What we supply

Stocked year-round, with seasonal peaks after each harvest. Large orders booked in advance.

🌾

Wheat straw

Bright, dry and long-fibred. The everyday bedding choice for cattle and horse yards.

🍺

Barley straw

Slightly softer and more absorbent. A favourite for calf pens and poultry houses.

🐎

Hay & haylage

Meadow hay and wrapped haylage for equestrian and smallholders. Cut from our own leys.

🔥

Biomass bales

Large Hesston bales for biomass boilers and AD plants. Consistent moisture, on-spec density.

🌱

Straw for growers

Mulch-grade straw for strawberry, soft-fruit and salad growers. Chopped on request.

📦

Small bales & sacks

Convenient 20kg bales and pre-packed sacks for smallholders, gardens and events.
